***Employee Attrition Analysis***

AnalystLab Africa – Data Science Internship (Week 1)

*Project Overview*

This project was completed as part of the Week 1 Data Science Internship Assignment at AnalystLab Africa. The objective was to perform an exploratory data analysis (EDA) on the IBM HR Analytics Employee Attrition dataset to understand workforce characteristics and identify factors that may contribute to employee attrition.

Rather than building a predictive model, the focus of this project is on understanding the business problem, exploring the data, and generating actionable insights that can support Human Resources (HR) decision-making.

*Business Problem*

Employee attrition can lead to increased recruitment costs, reduced productivity, and the loss of experienced employees. ABC Manufacturing Ltd wants to understand why employees leave the organization before investing in predictive machine learning solutions.
This project investigates workforce characteristics and explores relationships between employee attrition and various demographic, financial, and job-related factors.

*Objectives*

Understand the employee attrition problem from a business perspective.
Inspect and assess the quality of the dataset.
Perform exploratory data analysis (EDA).
Visualize workforce characteristics and attrition patterns.
Identify factors that may influence employee attrition.
Generate business-focused recommendations.

*Key Findings*

Most employees remain with the company, while approximately 16% have left.

Research & Development is the largest department and records the highest number of employees leaving.

Employees who work overtime are more likely to experience attrition.

Younger employees and those with lower monthly incomes show higher attrition.

Employees with shorter tenure and fewer total working years are more likely to leave.

Job role and department appear to influence employee turnover.
